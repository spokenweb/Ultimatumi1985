The Ultimatum Festival I, May 1985

Daniel Guimond at Ultimatum Festival 1985-05-01

The following is a transcription of Daniel Guimond’s performance at the Ultimatum Festival I, Wednesday, May 1st, 1985 at the Foufounes Électriques bar and performance space in Montréal, Quebec, Canada. Guimond performs poetry with underlying electric music, recorded sounds, and vocals in English and French. Guimond is a writer and multidisciplinary artist from Montréal. Some of his publications include the novel Pourquoi cela n'arrive qu'a moi ? (1999) and four poetry collections (1997, 1991, 1989, 1978). In his memoir High Friends in Low Places (2022), Alan Lord mentions Guimond’s punk zine SCRAP and his poetry which he performed while “being whipped” (p.52).

Alan Lord Commentary

Montreal poet Daniel Guimond was very provocative. In the late 70’s he gave performances dressed in S&M gear, reading his texts while being whipped. He also produced one of the first punk poezines, called SCRAP.
