Denis Vanier at Ultimatum Festival 1985-05-04 - Alan Lord Commentary

Alan Lord Commentary
Event Description

02:24

Here, as Vanier was reading, a female fan in the audience started heckling him. He said: “Are you coming up? You coming up or you’re scared? (meaning, to read in his place).” The woman came up, Vanier offered her his tome, and she brushed him off with: “I don’t need the book.” She finished the poem from memory, to great applause. Vanier came back to the microphone and thanked “Marie”.

Herbert Huncke at Ultimatum Festival 1985-05-05 - Alan Lord Commentary

Alan Lord Commentary
Event Description

00:00

Last on the festival bill—and closing off the proceedings—was beloved Beat Generation legend Herbert Huncke, reading from his book The Evening Sun Turned Crimson. I introduced Huncke, and he led off by saying: “I really want to start off with one particular story here, because I feel that it will fit into the general theme of the festival … a group of creative people, young people trying to do things, it’s very very encouraging for an old man like me… I want to think that things have progressed a little …”

Karl Jirgens at Ultimatum Festival 1985-05-05 - Alan Lord Commentary

Alan Lord Commentary
Event Description

00:00

Karl Jirgens is a writer, based in Toronto at the time, and was the editor of Rampike – a literary journal which featured hundreds of Canadian and other writers from around the world, also concrete poetry, found poetry, as well as interviews, from 1979 to 2016. He is also an 8th degree black-belt Grand-master in the martial art of Tae Kwon Do. During his performance he placed a strap over his head which held a revolver on each side of his head, pointing at his temples.
