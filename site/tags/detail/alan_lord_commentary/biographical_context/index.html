John Giorno Band at Ultimatum Festival 1985-05-05 - Alan Lord Commentary

Alan Lord Commentary
Biographical Context

00:00

John Giorno (December 4, 1936 – October 11, 2019) was an American poet and performance artist. He organized a number of early multimedia poetry experiments and events, notably his "Dial-A-Poem" project in the Sixties, which allowed people to call a phone number to hear brief poems by contemporary poets. He founded Giorno Poetry Systems in the Seventies, issuing vinyl albums and videos featuring himself and renowned artists such as William S. Burroughs, Patti Smith, Laurie Anderson, Philip Glass, Jim Carroll, Frank Zappa, Diamanda Galás, Allen Ginsberg, and John Cage. He was also a Buddhist, an AIDS activist, and later in life he made Poem Prints – multicolored paintings featuring pithy phrases such as “YOU GOTTA BURN TO SHINE”. In the Eighties he started touring with his John Giorno Band. The lineup here is: C.P. Roth – Music, keyboards , Adam Roth – Guitar , David Conrad – Bass , Mike Osborne - Drums

Karl Jirgens at Ultimatum Festival 1985-05-05 - Alan Lord Commentary

Alan Lord Commentary
Biographical Context

00:00

Karl Jirgens is a writer, based in Toronto at the time, and was the editor of Rampike – a literary journal which featured hundreds of Canadian and other writers from around the world, also concrete poetry, found poetry, as well as interviews, from 1979 to 2016. He is also an 8th degree black-belt Grand-master in the martial art of Tae Kwon Do. During his performance he placed a strap over his head which held a revolver on each side of his head, pointing at his temples.
